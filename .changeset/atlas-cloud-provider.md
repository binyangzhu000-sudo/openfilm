Atlas Cloud makes pictures (FLUX.2 Pro) and video clips with sound (Seedance 2.0, Seedance 2.0 Fast) with your own key: connect one in Studio (the OpenFilm logo at the top left → Providers) or set `ATLASCLOUD_API_KEY`, then choose Atlas Cloud for pictures or video, or run `openfilm get image --via atlascloud --out hero --prompt "…"` and `openfilm get video --via atlascloud --out shot --prompt "…" --duration 5`.
